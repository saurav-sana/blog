Inference Observability

September 12, 2026

Observing inference in production requires more than tracking latency and error rates. When optimizing local LLM development—such as running 7B to 14B parameter models like Gemma or Qwen on hardware constrained to 16GB of RAM—it demands a deep understanding of token generation bottlenecks, memory swapping, and queue depths at the model serving layer.
