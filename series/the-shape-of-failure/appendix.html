Mapping the Shape of Failure to LLM Infrastructure

December 5, 2026 · Appendix · AI/LLM Frontier

“All models are wrong, but some are useful.”

— George E. P. Box, Statistician

Welcome to the appendix. This is not a lookup table that prescribes a distribution merely because a system uses an LLM. That is statistical cargo-culting.

Instead, this is a translation guide. It maps the abstract mathematics of the previous eight parts to the observable, empirical realities of AI infrastructure. It is meant to be used as a decision aid: a way to verify changes, measure assumptions, and enforce system correctness when the underlying model is a black box.

The Golden Rule of AI SRE

When an AI system fails, the first question is never "Which distribution does this follow?" It is: "What physical process generated this observation, and what architectural control prevents it from taking down the system next time?"

The Verification Workflow

You cannot fix what you cannot measure, and you cannot measure what you cannot explain. When an AI system degrades, follow this deterministic workflow:

The Post-Mortem Protocol
  1. Observe: Do not look at averages. Look at p50, p95, and p99 latency. Look at the distribution of token lengths and context windows.
  2. Isolate: Where did the failure occur? Was it in the inference server (TTFT), the retrieval layer (empty context), or the agent parser (malformed JSON)?
  3. Identify the Mechanism: Map the failure to a physical reality. Was it a KV-cache OOM? Was it a compounding hallucination across 10 steps? Was it a thundering herd of retries?
  4. Apply the Control: Implement the specific architectural constraint (timeout, fallback, guardrail, temperature change) that prevents that mechanism from firing again.

The frontier of AI infrastructure requires more rigor than traditional distributed systems, not less. A standard database either writes the row or it doesn't. An LLM might write a row that looks plausible, but is entirely hallucinated. Use the math. Trust the telemetry. And always build the circuit breaker.
