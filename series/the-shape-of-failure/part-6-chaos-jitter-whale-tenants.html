Part 6: Chaos, Jitter, and Whale Tenants

August 24, 2026

In any shared infrastructure processing global trade repositories, "whale tenants"—those contributing massively disproportional data volumes compared to the median—introduce dangerous queue jitter.

Without strict partitioning strategies in underlying stores like Cosmos DB, these tenants can starve smaller clients during complex OTC derivative reconciliation tasks.
